Today was our first league came against Bletchley Scot’s, it was a good battle between both teams.

Today was a game of errors and it just wasn’t going our way today at all, but I have very happy with alot of the football we produced, every team has them sort of games, that’s just what happens in football, good thing is its things we can learn from and go in with confidence on the next and put this game behind us.

⭐ Final 2025–26 highlight

2 May 2026 · Panthers 5–3 Buckingham FC Youth U9 Rovers

We played away today on this bright sunny morning at Buckingham, both teams played very well and both looked very organised. Buckingham defended our chances really well and moved the ball around nicely on the attack. Panthers struggled a bit today with only having a squad of 7 and also having to switch goalkeepers, but this didn’t stop our mighty panthers, we created a lot of chances and started very strong but just struggled to get the ball in the net. A huge shoutout to all the outfield players who took the courage to put the gloves on and go in goal. We saw some very good movement and passing from the panthers, we were lacking in communication today but this can always be worked on. Still a very good solid performance to finish off on our last league game of the season, hopefully a well deserved promotion from a great second half of the season. Player of the match today goes to prajen, who scored some very good striker goals today and gave us the attack we needed infront of goal. Special mention to Rylan, who really attacked with great force and was always looking to put the ball through to other players. Massive well done to the panthers another great season locked in, bring on the tournaments and next season ❤️⚽️
